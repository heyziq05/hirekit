Data Analyst - FinTech Payments (Kuala Lumpur)

About the role
We are looking for a Data Analyst to join our Payments Analytics team. You will turn transaction data into insights that help product and operations teams make decisions.

Responsibilities
- Build and maintain dashboards in Power BI to track payment success rates, fraud and customer retention
- Write complex SQL queries to extract and transform data from our data warehouse (Snowflake)
- Perform A/B testing analysis and present findings to stakeholders
- Automate recurring reports using Python (pandas)
- Partner with product managers and engineers to define KPIs
- Ensure data quality and documentation of data pipelines

Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Statistics, Computer Science, Mathematics or related field
- 2+ years of experience as a Data Analyst
- Strong SQL skills and experience with Power BI or Tableau
- Experience with Python (pandas) for data analysis
- Understanding of statistics and A/B testing
- Excellent communication skills and ability to present to stakeholders

Nice to have
- Experience in payments, banking or fintech
- Knowledge of Snowflake or dbt
- Familiarity with fraud detection
