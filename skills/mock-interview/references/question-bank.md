# Question bank

Starting points, not a script. Adapt every question to the job description and the candidate's resume. A question about their actual project beats a generic one every time.

## Contents
- Openers
- HR / recruiter screen
- Behavioural (hiring manager)
- Gap and pressure questions
- Technical / role-specific, by role family
- Closing

## Openers
- Tell me about yourself. (Expect 60–90 seconds: present → past → why this role.)
- Walk me through your resume.
- What do you know about our company?
- Why are you interested in this role?

## HR / recruiter screen
- Why are you looking to leave your current role? / Why did you leave your last job?
- Why this company, and why now?
- What are you looking for in your next role?
- What are your salary expectations? (Good answers give a researched range or ask about the band first. Malaysia: often asked as "expected salary" with current salary. Note whether they handle it calmly.)
- What is your notice period / when could you start?
- Are you open to [hybrid / relocation / travel] as described in the posting?
- Where do you see yourself in 3–5 years?
- Are you interviewing elsewhere?
- For fresh graduates: Why did you choose your degree? Tell me about your final-year project / internship.

## Behavioural (hiring manager)
Expect STAR answers (situation, task, action, result). Pick the ones that match the job's requirements.
- Tell me about a time you achieved a result you're proud of.
- Tell me about a time you had to meet a tight deadline.
- Describe a time you disagreed with a teammate or manager. What happened?
- Tell me about a mistake you made and what you learned.
- Tell me about a time you had to learn something new quickly.
- Describe a time you handled several priorities at once.
- Tell me about a time you used data to make a decision.
- Tell me about a time you influenced someone without authority.
- Describe a difficult customer / stakeholder and how you handled them.
- Tell me about a time you led something, even informally.
- Tell me about a time something failed. What did you do?

## Gap and pressure questions
Use for real gaps between the job and the resume, and for tough-mode pressure.
- This role needs [X]. I don't see it on your resume. How would you handle that part of the job?
- Your experience is mostly in [industry A]; we're [industry B]. Why should we take that risk?
- I see a gap between [date] and [date]. What were you doing?
- You've changed jobs fairly often. Why?
- What's your biggest weakness? (Good: a real, non-fatal weakness plus what they're doing about it.)
- Why should we hire you over other candidates?
- If you got this job, what would you focus on in your first 90 days?

## Technical / role-specific, by role family
Pitch the level to the job (junior vs senior). Ask one, then follow up on their answer rather than firing a list.

**Software / data engineering**
- Explain how you'd design a REST API for [feature from the job]. How would you handle errors, versioning, auth?
- Walk me through how [a project on their resume] works end to end. Why those technology choices?
- How would you find and fix a slow database query?
- What happens when you deploy? How would you roll back a bad release?
- Write or describe an approach for [a small problem tied to the domain, e.g. find the nearest available rider].
- How do you test your code? Unit vs integration tests?

**Data analysis / BI**
- How would you measure whether [a feature/campaign from the job's domain] worked?
- Write the SQL for [a realistic question: top 5 customers by revenue last month].
- How do you design an A/B test? What can go wrong?
- A dashboard number suddenly drops 30%. How do you investigate?

**Marketing / digital marketing**
- You have RM[budget] for next month's campaign for [product]. How would you split it and why?
- ROAS dropped from 4 to 2 this week. What do you check first?
- How would you plan a campaign for [mega-sale / product launch from the job]?
- How do you choose and brief KOLs / influencers, and measure their impact?

**Finance / accounting**
- Walk me through the month-end close process you followed.
- How would you investigate a variance between budget and actual?
- Explain [a standard relevant to the job, e.g. MFRS 15 revenue recognition] simply.

**Sales / business development / customer success**
- Sell me [the company's product] in 60 seconds.
- Walk me through your sales process from lead to close.
- A key client is unhappy and threatening to leave. What do you do?

**Operations / project management**
- How do you plan a project with a fixed deadline and unclear requirements?
- A supplier/vendor misses a critical date. What do you do?
- Which metrics would you track to run [the operation in the job]?

**Healthcare / education / other**
- Use scenario questions from the job description: "A [patient/student/customer] does X. What do you do?" Follow up on safety, policy and communication.

## Closing
- Do you have any questions for us? (No questions at all is a red flag. Good questions are about the team, success in the role, challenges, and next steps.)
- Is there anything else you'd like us to know?
